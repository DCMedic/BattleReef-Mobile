## Subtitle / short description

Smarter aquarium care, offline first.

## Full description

BattleReef is a standalone aquarium-management app designed to make consistent, informed aquarium care easier without requiring dedicated controller hardware or a permanent cloud connection.

Track multiple aquariums, record water parameters, define custom target ranges, maintain livestock and equipment inventories, schedule recurring care, and build a complete husbandry timeline. BattleReef turns those records into transparent stability, trend, change, and advisory insights that explain the evidence behind what the app surfaces.

Create longitudinal aquarium and livestock photo histories with guided capture conditions and before/after comparisons. Your records remain locally useful offline, and BattleReef provides user-controlled backup, restore, and CSV export tools for data portability.

Beta capabilities include:

- Multiple independent aquarium profiles
- Manual water-test logging and configurable targets
- Deterministic stability and trend analysis
- Explainable aquarium advisories
- Husbandry and maintenance timeline
- Recurring local care reminders
- Livestock and equipment lifecycle records
- Aquarium and livestock visual histories
- Guided photo capture and comparison
- Full local backup and defensive restore
- CSV water-test export
- Offline-first operation

BattleReef does not directly control aquarium equipment in this release. Future BattleReef Marine Controller integration will be introduced separately when compatible hardware enters production.
